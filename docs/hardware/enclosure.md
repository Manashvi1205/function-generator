# Hardware and enclosure notes

## Front panel

The report specifies an enclosure approximately **200 mm wide × 80 mm high × 240 mm
deep**, with:

- 64 × 64 mm main display cutout;
- 11 mm knob ports;
- 28.8 × 26 mm power input opening;
- 10 mm diameter push buttons;
- two BNC outputs, ground, and an on/off switch;
- keypad, OLED, additional controls, potentiometers, and an internal wire-routing slot.

The final design used clear acrylic with an angled front panel, ventilation cutouts, and
dedicated connector/control openings. Acrylic sheets were laser cut from an RDWorks
layout, heat bent, and chemically joined with chloroform. A later redesign improved
structural stability, component space, cable management, and access.

## Documented checks

Before fabrication, the team considered:

- display-cutout alignment;
- connector and switch space;
- wiring and mounting clearance;
- enclosure stability;
- display visibility for multiple students;
- fit of the final target unit.

The report says a thermocol model was used to explore front-panel angles and records a
60-degree proposal. It does not provide a final dimensioned CAD file.

## Source availability

No CAD source, laser-cut vector, printable model, dimensioned drawing, schematic, PCB,
or Gerber file is included in the delivered package. This document transcribes report
claims and is not fabrication-ready.
